# Hum — the Task Manager that explains itself

> **Listen to your computer.** A Task Manager that tells you what's going on, not just the numbers.

Hum shows what's happening on your computer, explains why, and lets you act in one click: what's draining your battery, which app isn't responding, and what's blocking a disk from ejecting.

Hum is made for **Mac and Windows**, with the same look and feel on both. The Mac app comes first; the Windows app follows as its own native release.

🚧 **Work in progress.** Hum is in its design phase. There's nothing to download yet.

## Principles

- **Answer first, data second.** Every screen starts by telling you if everything's fine.
- **Every problem comes with an action.** Close, force quit, eject — right next to the issue.
- **Plain language.** No jargon, in English and Spanish.
- **Light.** Hum shouldn't use what it warns you about.
- **Private.** No telemetry, no network, everything stays on your computer.
